Laboratory Director

Prof. Sung-Ju Park (박성주 교수)

Assistant Professor, School of Electrical & Control Engineering & Department of Naval Architecture & Ocean Engineering, Tongmyong University

Biography

Prof. Park studied naval architecture at Tongmyong University (B.S., 2014) and trained in fracture mechanics under Prof. Joonmo Choung at Inha University (M.S., 2016; Ph.D., 2020), with theses on the three-dimensional fracture strain surface of Arctic steel EH36 and on ductile fracture for shell-element analysis. After a postdoctoral position at Inha University, he joined Tongmyong University in 2020, where he leads the Applied Cyber-Physical Systems Laboratory.

His work links physical testing, simulation and AI so that results from the laboratory can be used on real ships and offshore structures.

Career Milestone Summary

From material tests to digital twins

One question has run through this work since 2013: how do marine structures behave and fail, and how early can we predict it? Each phase builds on the one before. The material database and calibrated fracture models of Phase 1 underpin the naval survivability simulations of Phase 2, and those validated simulations become the physics and training data for the AI surrogates and digital twins of Phase 3. Earlier phases continue alongside the new ones.

Phase 1 · Foundation since 2013

Experiment- and simulation-based structural integrity

실험 및 해석 기반 구조 건전성 평가
Extreme-environment material database
Structural steels and composites tested at cryogenic temperature, high strain rate and under hydrogen charging, on purpose-built rigs.
Modelling & simulation
User-subroutine nonlinear fracture and damage analysis, applied to collision, grounding and marine-accident risk.

Key paper: Ductile fracture prediction of EH36 grade steel based on Hosford–Coulomb model Ships and Offshore Structures, 2019

Phase 2 · Expansion since 2020

Integrated survivability of naval ships

함정 통합 생존성 평가
Stealth performance
Structure–acoustic coupled analysis to predict and reduce underwater radiated noise and target strength.
Survivability assessment
Verification and validation of M&S, and an integrated simulator for anti-ship missile hit points and vulnerability.

Key paper: Experimental procedure for target strength measurement in a small water tank using cumulative energy method Int. J. Naval Architecture and Ocean Engineering, 2026

Phase 3 · Future innovation since 2023

AI and digital-twin intelligent monitoring

AI·디지털 트윈 융합 지능형 모니터링
AI-based monitoring
Data augmentation and physics-informed neural networks (PINN/DNN) that make physics models fast enough for design and monitoring.
Digital-twin simulators
Unity and MuJoCo–ROS2 simulators that synchronise virtual and real systems for real-time monitoring and remote control.

Key paper: A physics-informed neural network framework integrating engineering codes for design optimization of stiffened cylinders Thin-Walled Structures, 2026
