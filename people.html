Biography

Prof. Park received his B.S. (2008–2014) in naval architecture from Tongmyong University and his M.S. (2014–2016) and Ph.D. (2016–2020) in naval architecture and ocean engineering from Inha University, specializing in structural mechanics and fracture analysis. After a postdoctoral appointment at Inha University (2020) and serving on the faculty of Tongmyong University (2020–2027), he joined the Department of Artificial Intelligence & Defense Engineering at Changwon National University, where he leads the Applied Cyber-Physical Systems Laboratory.

Career Milestone Summary

From material tests to digital twins

One question has run through this work since 2013: how do marine structures behave and fail, and how early can we predict it? Each phase builds on the one before. The material database and calibrated fracture models of Phase 1 underpin the underwater acoustics and signature control simulations of Phase 2, and those validated simulations become the physics and training data for the AI surrogates and digital twins of Phase 3. Earlier phases continue alongside the new ones.

Laboratory Members & Recruitment

Graduate & Undergraduate Researchers

Join the lab →

Student researchers at ACPS Lab work directly on national defense, research institute and shipyard projects — combining physical testing, numerical simulation and AI surrogate modeling, with student awards at national autonomous vessel competitions (KABOAT) and academic conferences (see student awards). We are actively recruiting founding M.S., Ph.D. and undergraduate researchers in the Department of Artificial Intelligence & Defense Engineering at Changwon National University.

Track 01 · Structures

Extreme Structural Mechanics

High-speed, cryogenic and hydrogen-charged material testing, user-defined constitutive subroutines and nonlinear finite element collapse analysis for naval and offshore structures.

Track 02 · Acoustics & Stealth

Underwater Acoustics (TS & URN)

GPU ray-shooting target strength (TS) prediction, vibro-acoustic underwater radiated noise (URN) modeling, air-bubble curtain masking and resilient mount characterization.

Track 03 · AI & Digital Twins

Physics-Informed AI & Autonomy

Physics-informed neural networks (PINNs) for structural design optimization and real-time 3D digital twins for autonomous surface and underwater vehicles.
