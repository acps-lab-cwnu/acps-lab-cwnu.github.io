Opportunities

Graduate students

M.S. / Ph.D.

Participate in funded defense, national institute and shipyard R&D projects in structural mechanics, underwater acoustics (TS & URN), physics-informed AI and digital twins. Tuition support, monthly research stipends and conference travel are provided.

Undergraduates

Research interns

Gain hands-on experience in finite element simulation, AI surrogate modeling and autonomous vessel experiments such as the Korea Autonomous Boat Competition (KABOAT). Open to AI, Defense, Mechanical, Naval Architecture and Electrical/Computer majors.

How to apply

Send an email

Email your CV, academic transcript and a brief note on your interests to the address below. Informal inquiries and lab visits prior to formal admission are always welcome.
