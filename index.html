Core Pillars

Research Scope & Strategic Focus

Connecting multi-axial material testing, multiphysics finite element simulation, acoustic physics, and AI surrogate models for future defense and marine platforms.

Laboratory Research Statement

CPSS Lab addresses critical engineering challenges in cyber-physical marine structural systems, naval stealth physics, and intelligent digital twin control. By coupling multi-axial material testing with high-fidelity FE simulations and physics-informed AI, we deliver reliable solutions for naval combatants and subsea infrastructure.

Structural Integrity & Extreme Materials

3D ductile fracture strain surfaces (Hosford-Coulomb), cryogenic (-197°C) & high-strain rate material DB, low-cycle fatigue, and hydrogen embrittlement evaluation.

Read More →

Naval Stealth & Acoustic Physics

Underwater radiated noise (URN) bubble layer insertion loss, target strength (TS) measurement, radar cross section (RCS), and anti-ship missile hit location algorithms.

Read More →

Physics-Informed AI & Digital Twins

Physics-Informed Neural Networks (PINN) for stiffened cylinder optimization, submarine pressure hull collapse strength prediction, and Unity-ROS2 virtual control.
