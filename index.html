Cyber-Physical Systems Laboratory

사이버물리시스템 연구실

A cyber-physical system couples a physical asset with the computation that senses, models and controls it. We build that coupling for ships, submarines and offshore structures: material and acoustic experiments define the physics, high-fidelity simulation and physics-informed AI make it fast enough to use, and digital twins put it to work on real platforms.

Our work is funded by the Defense Acquisition Program Administration, the Republic of Korea Navy, Hanwha Ocean, HD Hyundai Heavy Industries, KRISO and NRF.

How the work connects

Sense. Material tests, water-tank acoustics and field sensors capture what the physical system actually does.

Model. Finite element, acoustic and electromagnetic simulations turn those data into validated physics models.

Learn. Physics-informed neural networks compress the models into surrogates fast enough for design decisions.

Twin. Digital twins close the loop, feeding live data back to monitor, control and operate marine systems.
